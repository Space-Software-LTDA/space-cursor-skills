We use a 12 column system with a gutter (space between two columns) of 16px.
Grid system
Columns
We will asume that the grid works the same way it does in the Bootstrap framework.
Columns are enclosed within containers. These are the containers for each breakpoints:
sm: 540px,  md: 720px, lg: 960px, xl: 1140px.
